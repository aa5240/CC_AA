"Creative Coding Is..."

The Earthly Delights, its semesterly beginnings “An algorithm requires assumptions, an algorithm requires decisions” (Form+Code in Design, Art, and Architecture by Casey Reas, Chandler McWilliams,LUST. Pg 13) Creative coding/creative code is an emergence of linguistic techniques activated in functions of machine learning to develop visual scapes with hopes to further understand how equations can calculate our existence. It's a collective understanding that can be obsolete if time takes its toll on human creation, yet its principles are what flourish from electrical particle exchange. The formation of written words to colorful sequences. To understand as to create, to develop and inspire a continuous pathway of technical brushstrokes able to sustain its physically classically trained predecessors. An experience of the past, present and future.
