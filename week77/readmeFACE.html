Facial Archive with Echo and Sound

The concept was clear, I wanted to include my personal work as it moved with my face in space, through rings. Translating what I see with My eyes into a mask, into what my eyes saw, face felt, ears heard. I wanted it to too engage with the sense as they are attached to my Face. I wanted the stories lived to become a mask, a collage of some sorts changing colors as it rotates around the screen. I wanted my MP3 files to play since they are spatial sounds and develop further spatial recognition. Unfortunately I was unable to add them, or they did not load, so I decided to look for ways to synthesis audio and I found echo and delays, I did however ask the LLM to update them and Visual Studio to debate what was going wrong through the process of displaying videos on each pint of facemesh. I also do not know how well my face was captured, but I did appreciate having my mouth become an instrument with moving rings, creating echoes all around and trying to create a circle, but stopping middle of the way. I did have to ask for help through the process, I would say On my end I will take time to further figure out the aspects of using My face as a digital archive holder. I would like to experiment further with this body of work I created, and hopefully it can be assisted further without needing to use LLMs or AI to figure out what updates are needed.
