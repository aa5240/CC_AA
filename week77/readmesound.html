Sounding off to the Archive

The goal is/was to create a sound scape of 3D objects going in an orbit circle for 30 minutes on catching hems, oops and mantra sounds. The orbit rotates and the audience can engage with seeing particles move in a peace note, tones moving all around them and particles shifting with bass, treble; catching frequency sequences and moving all around the screen as each frequency performs in shapes and appears beyond what's on the surface. Moving forward into creating distortions in already existing p5,j shapes that can gain texture from videos I uploaded for a personal project of floating footage I have taken with my camera. I wanted to re-create a project I have performed with code to perform a sequence written in code and can merge with sound. The concept I wanted to follow was a serene concept of Utopia, shapes distorting with shaders, helped by LLM; turning them into fragments changing color as they move towards a key, frequency or note. The sound piece was created into a 4 channel spatial sound, so I wanted code to reflect it on the screen, I took this mantra “Jhené Aiko - alive & well (gratitude mantra)” Made it a 24 minute beating sound that repeated and lingered into space. So we can visualize frequencies shifting slowly and distorting ion 3D to create a performance of the mantra in real time. .
