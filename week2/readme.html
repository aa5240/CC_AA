CUBEST-FACE

CUBEST FACE! Takes from the understanding of what Cubism can be: The Cubist painters rejected the inherited concept that art should copy nature, or that they should adopt the traditional techniques of perspective, modeling, and foreshortening. I utilize it as a form of introduction to the work I would tend to create whenever I am learning or relearning a medium to create art from. The eyes flair and move as the colors darken. The nature shapes pixelate as every click is pressed and circles format as the intention deepens. A move of the mouse, a flecker of color and a 1980s palate to glycine as the piece is moving and recreating..
